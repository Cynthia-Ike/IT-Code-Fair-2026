The Long Way to Care

Can you call for help from a remote Northern Territory community?

And how far away is the nearest care?

Mobile coverage records, road-network drive times and clinic contact details for 782 remote communities, brought together in one place. Search any community to see whether a call for help is likely to connect, how long the drive is to a clinic and a hospital, and who to phone. Search a clinic to get its number and hours directly.

    About this data: method, limits and ethics

    How the numbers were built

    • Coverage combines a 2021 community list, a 2022 list of covered sites and 2025 tower registers from all four carriers. The most direct and recent evidence wins.
    • Drive times come from a routing pass over Geoscience Australia's national road network (48,391 road segments), supplied to this project as input data.
    • The Gap Index is the equal-weighted average of four percentile-ranked risks: no confirmed coverage, clinic drive time, hospital drive time and no sealed road.
    • A community with no mapped road is scored as maximum risk, not dropped. It is the exact case the index exists to surface.
    • Risk tiers are quartiles of the Gap Index. Alternative weightings were tested and flag the same top 20 communities.

    What this can't prove

    • "Never recorded" means no list found a signal there, not that there is none. The NT Government calls its lists a guide only.
    • "Tower within 15 km" is a modelling assumption about a macro cell's reach on flat land, so it is kept as its own category.
    • Drive times assume dry-season roads. Wet-season closures make trips longer or impossible.
    • The fastest drive may be to a different health service than the listed remote clinic whose phone number is shown. The result card names both.
    • This is a class prototype built on public data, not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 000.

    Ethics and community

    • No personal or health records are used. Every score describes a place's access to services, not the people who live there.
    • Outstations are homelands on Country. The gap measured is in the infrastructure that reaches them, not in the communities.
    • Missing data is shown as missing, never quietly filled in.
    • Coverage reports collect a place, a status and an optional note. No names or contact details, and nothing leaves this device unless you send the code yourself.
